Cover letter — submission to AIP Advances

Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript "Oxidation-budgeted design of lead-free RbGeI3
solar cells: back-surface-field enhancement, realistic loss budgeting and
SHAP sensitivity from SCAPS-1D optimization to manufacturing tolerances,
benchmarked against CsGeI3" for consideration as a Regular Article in
AIP Advances.

Lead-halide perovskites dominate single-junction efficiencies but carry a
lead-toxicity liability. Using SCAPS-1D on one frozen parameter deck, we
optimize a lead-free FTO/TiO2/RbGeI3/CuI/Au cell to 27.06% PCE and go
beyond standard parameter sweeps: a Cu2O-like back-surface field (28.01%
ideal; +0.98 pp on the identical full-realism stack), a single stacked
loss waterfall bounding the experimentally plausible window (20.78%,
21.76% with BSF), a defect-energy and capture-asymmetry mapping of
Ge-related degradation converted into a quantitative oxidation fab spec
(E^f >= 0.46 eV at 300 K equilibrium), manufacturing tolerances for
series/shunt resistance, doping, and mobility, a held-out XGBoost/SHAP
sensitivity analysis, a same-stack CsGeI3 gap control, and a calibration
check of the solver pipeline against two experimental beta-CsPbI3
devices reproduced within 2%. The work gives experimentalists
quantitative targets (BSF NA ~1e19 cm-3, bulk Nt <= 1e15 cm-3, mobility
>= 15 cm2/Vs) rather than an idealized efficiency number.

The manuscript fits the broad applied-physics and energy-materials scope
of AIP Advances. All simulations are documented for reproducibility
(Supplementary Sections S1–S7 with decks, scripts, and archived result
files), and every performance claim sits below its Shockley–Queisser
limit with the approximations disclosed in the text.

The work is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. All
authors approve submission. We suggest reviewers with expertise in
lead-free perovskite simulation and SCAPS-1D device modelling.
