Cover letter — submission to Solar Energy (Elsevier)

Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript "Defect-Tolerant Design of Lead-Free RbGeI3 Solar
Cells: From SCAPS-1D Optimization to Manufacturing Tolerances, Benchmarked
Against CsGeI3" for consideration as a Regular Paper in Solar Energy.

Using SCAPS-1D, we optimize a lead-free FTO/TiO2/RbGeI3/CuI/Au cell to
26.81% PCE and go beyond standard parameter sweeps: defect-energy/
capture-asymmetry mapping of Ge-related degradation, background-doping and
mobility viability windows, series-resistance manufacturing tolerances,
activation-energy diagnostics, and a full same-stack CsGeI3 comparison
isolating the A-site cation effect. The work gives experimentalists
quantitative targets (doping <= 1e15 cm-3, mobility >= 15 cm2/Vs,
Rs <= 8 ohm.cm2) rather than an idealized efficiency number.

The work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors
approve submission. We suggest reviewers with expertise in lead-free
perovskite simulation and SCAPS-1D device modelling.
