Cover letter — submission to Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells

Dear Editor,

We submit our revised manuscript "Oxidation-Budgeted Design of Lead-Free
RbGeI3 Solar Cells: Back-Surface-Field Enhancement, Realistic Loss Budgeting
and SHAP Sensitivity From SCAPS-1D Optimization to Manufacturing Tolerances,
Benchmarked Against CsGeI3" for reconsideration as a Regular Paper in
Solar Energy.

Using SCAPS-1D on one frozen deck, we optimize a lead-free
FTO/TiO2/RbGeI3/CuI/Au cell to 27.06% PCE (SQ-checked) and go beyond
standard parameter sweeps: a Cu2O-like p+ back-surface field raising the
ideal point to 28.01% (+0.98 pp on the identical full-realism stack,
20.78% to 21.76%, via rear-interface gap widening isolated by controlled
trap attribution with a p+ control), a D2/NiO cross-check through the same
pipeline (28.16%, so the reported stack trades efficiency for stability),
a single stacked loss waterfall bounding the experimentally plausible window
(optics, bulk/interface defects, radiative/Auger bounds, parasitics),
defect-energy/capture-asymmetry mapping of Ge-related degradation,
background-doping and mobility viability windows recomputed on the
stacked point, series/shunt manufacturing tolerances, temperature
diagnostics with a disclosed Ea extrapolation artifact (1.48 eV), a
lifetime baseline (1000 ns, 8.6 um) for experimental comparison, a
held-out XGBoost/SHAP sensitivity analysis recovering bulk defects,
bandgap and electron mobility as the top levers, and a same-stack CsGeI3
gap control (fixed-chi protocol, not A-site proof). The work gives
experimentalists quantitative targets (BSF NA ~1e19 cm-3, bulk Nt <=
1e15 cm-3, mobility >= 15 cm2/Vs, Rs <= 4 ohm.cm2) rather than an
idealized efficiency number.

Crucially, this revision answers the experimental-grounding concern head-on.
No RbGeI3 device has been fabricated to date, so we calibration-check the
solver pipeline itself: our SCAPS-1D rebuild of the experimental beta-CsPbI3
devices of Wang et al. (Science 2019) reproduces both the control
(15.18 vs 15.10%) and the CHI-passivated champion (18.49 vs 18.40%)
within 2% by tuning defect densities (plus one resistive correction),
presented as calibration rather than validation. We further place our
realistic window against a frozen pre-2021 snapshot of 41,316 reported
devices from the Perovskite Database Project (NOMAD) purely as era-bounded
context, with no superiority claim over the present state of the art.
Finally, we convert the central liability of this absorber --- Ge2+
oxidation --- into a quantitative fab spec: mapping V_Ge vacancy
thermodynamics onto our defect ladder shows E^f >= 0.46 eV (300 K
equilibrium; 0.57-0.65 eV for anneal freeze-in) holds 27.06% while ambient
processing falls below 20.76%, with passivation chemistries (PCCMAI, Lewis
bases) acting as Nt reduction in waterfall language.

The work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors
approve submission. A prior version was rejected; this revision rebuilds
the contribution around the oxidation budget, BSF variant with measured
desensitization mechanism, single stacked loss budget, lifetime baseline,
held-out ML surrogate, and experimental anchoring. We suggest
reviewers with expertise in lead-free perovskite simulation and SCAPS-1D
device modelling.
